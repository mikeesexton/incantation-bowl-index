## Method decisions now fixed

- One row per physical object, with publication aliases stored separately.
- Separate confidence fields for reading, translation, date, provenance, hand, identity, and religious attribution.
- Publisher or repository metadata may verify bibliographic facts and declared arguments, but not line-level readings.
- Script is evidence for a scribal tradition, not automatic proof of a client's or practitioner's religion.
- "Origin" claims require a proposed direction and route of transmission; thematic resemblance alone is insufficient.
- Unprovenanced objects may support philology but carry reduced weight for household, depositional, geographic, and frequency claims.
