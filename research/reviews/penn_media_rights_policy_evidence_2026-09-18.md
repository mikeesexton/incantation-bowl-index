# Penn Museum media-rights policy evidence cohort — 18 September 2026

This note extends the institutional-policy evidence work for `RIGHTS-002` to
the Penn Museum cohort. It scopes policy evidence for 288 media rows attached
to 288 objects and appearances, representing 280 distinct image URLs. It does
**not** make a legal determination, decide that an image is reusable, or
complete an individual rights assessment. Every row remains `needs_review` and
fail closed in the public projection.

## Cohort and method

- The 288 records use direct Penn Museum collection image URLs ending in
  `_800.jpg`; their linked source pages are Penn collection object records.
- The current official Penn Museum policy, terms, collections-data guidance
  and rights contact page were checked on 18 September 2026.
- The policy review distinguishes permission stated for collection images from
  the separate Creative Commons license for downloaded collections metadata.
- No automated inference was made from the institution, URL pattern, source
  type, source-level rights label or an object's presence in the collection.

## Current official policy evidence

Penn Museum's current
[website terms and conditions](https://www.penn.museum/about/statements-and-policies/terms-and-conditions)
state that collection-object images and downloadable scans may be used for
non-profit, educational or personal purposes unless otherwise noted. The terms
limit the files to research, educational or personal use, require a Penn Museum
object-number credit and a link to `https://www.penn.museum` for digital use,
require modifications to be identified, exclude high-resolution or
publication-quality files, and prohibit commercial use.

The Museum's
[rights and permissions page](https://www.penn.museum/about-collections/rights-and-permissions)
directs requests for high-resolution or publication-quality images and
permission to `photos@pennmuseum.org`, while referring use of images already
downloadable from the online collections back to the website terms. The
[collections-data download page](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/objects/data.php)
applies CC BY 4.0 to the downloadable datasets and expressly excludes images.
The metadata license must therefore not be copied onto the image rows.

These terms provide a concrete basis for human review of a narrowly scoped
non-commercial research or educational display. They do not by themselves
establish that the project's present CC BY 4.0 public-data license, or a future
paid service, may redistribute the images. That compatibility question remains
for human rights review or an item/cohort-specific permission request.

media rows and therefore need one coherent resource-level disposition:

| Image URL suffix | Media rows |

The public exporter already fails closed when copies of an exact URL have
conflicting or incomplete dispositions. The shared-resource groups should be
reviewed together rather than approved independently.

## Questions for human review or Penn Museum

1. Do the direct `_800.jpg` files in this cohort count as collection images
   downloadable from the online collections under the current terms?
2. Would a public scholarly reference interface qualify as the stated
   non-profit research or educational use, and may the files be redistributed
   as part of that interface rather than merely displayed from Penn's service?
3. Are any of the 280 resources subject to object-specific notices, third-party
   photographer rights or other exceptions not visible in the shared policy?
4. Is the required credit consistently the object-number form in the terms, or
   does any image require a photographer, donor or collection credit as well?
5. Can Penn grant broader public and commercial reuse permission if the project
   is to include images in a CC BY data release or a future paid service?

## Current disposition

Policy evidence now covers the 288-row Penn cohort, but completed individual
