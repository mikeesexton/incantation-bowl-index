- Evidence binding: media fingerprint
  `ccd07d96446bccb9c26c30cbcc3a8ddb65c72b2b5c5b8577ad84d1a2829416d1`;
  complete held volume SHA-256
  `2d087410e67656b16aa7e92468e8c0e78149edc671c285bb280d24416f741dcd`.
- Ford 2023 printed p. 218 (PDF page 228) reproduces the bowl as figure 1 and
  captions it as Davidovitz 41. The figure page does not name a photographer or
  state a media license.
- The volume's copyright page (PDF page 4) reserves rights to the Jack, Joseph
  and Morton Mandel Institute of Jewish Studies and to the article authors. It
  does not separately identify ownership of figure 1.
- The Hebrew University's current general [terms of use](https://new.huji.ac.il/terms-use)
  cover images unless expressly stated otherwise and direct copyright questions
  to `Copyright@mail.huji.ac.il`. A general site policy is not itself proof that
  the university owns this particular photograph.
- Follow-up: ask the institute/publisher or the named copyright contact who
  created figure 1, whether the Davidovitz Collection or another party controls
  it, and what permission and attribution would cover the intended use.
