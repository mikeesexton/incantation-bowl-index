# Montgomery independent reading-text review packet

Prepared 20 September 2026 for **TEXT-006**. This is a request for independent
review, not a completed scholarly certification and not a request to choose a
canonical Aramaic reading.

## The bounded ask

Please compare the index's normalized English reading text with James A.
Montgomery's printed English translation in *Aramaic Incantation Texts from
Nippur* (1913). The complete public-domain scan is available from
[Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/aramaicincantati00mont); the exact
PDF reviewed by the project has SHA-256
`c85f9eaadcd910652543bb54dab16e5a1a4ac7883df7b433ea2c9aa0b357abdf`.

The first-pass texts normalize line wrapping, line-end hyphenation, paragraph
spacing, some punctuation spacing, small capitals and ordinary Latin-name
diacritics. They are not diplomatic transcriptions and do not claim to verify
the underlying Aramaic. Please concentrate on:

1. personal, divine and angelic names;
2. Latin-letter magical formulae and their diacritics;
3. gaps, brackets, restorations, uncertainty marks and `sic`;
4. omitted, duplicated or reordered printed wording; and
5. places where normalization could conceal a meaningful source distinction.

For each item, report one outcome: `verified`, `verified_with_note`,
`correction_proposed`, or `unable_to_assess`. For every note or proposed
correction, please give the printed page, the affected phrase, the proposed
reading and a short reason. A proposed correction will not overwrite the
current row; the project will preserve both review states and apply any accepted
revision through its append-only proofreading ledger.

## Intake boundary

The reviewer should return observations, not edit the dataset. Project intake
will verify the cited page and current text hash, distinguish a source correction
from an interpretive disagreement, and create a new proofreading manifest if a
revision is warranted. Script/language, identity, authenticity and public-release
decisions remain separate from this review.
