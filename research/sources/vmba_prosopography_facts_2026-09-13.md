# VMBA prosopography — recovered sample, factual columns only

## What this is

Fifty records recovered from the **Prosopography of the Babylonian Magic Bowls**,
compiled by Dr Ortal-Paz Saar for a BIRAX project directed by Dr Dan Levene and
Prof. Gideon Bohak, formerly hosted at the University of Southampton.

The database is offline: the SharePoint host has been decommissioned. Only one page
of the list view survives in the Internet Archive, which is why this is 50 records
and not the roughly 1,800 the record IDs imply.

- Source: `https://sharepoint.soton.ac.uk/sites/vmba/Lists/Prosopography2/VMBA.aspx`
  (offline; recovered from the Internet Archive snapshot of 15 March 2012)
- Project page: https://digitalhumanities.soton.ac.uk/projects/magic-bowls

## What is withheld, and why

The original has nineteen columns. **Four are withheld here** because they carry
Dr Saar's scholarly expression rather than fact, and no publication basis is on
record — not public domain, not an open licence, not permission:

| Withheld column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `etymological_notes` | Argued etymologies, with attribution to the scholars proposing them |
| `contents_of_incantation` | Her summary of each incantation's purpose |
| `notes` | Corrections to earlier scholarship |
| `open_questions` | Unresolved problems she identified |

The sixteen columns in the CSV are facts about the objects and their bibliography:
names, sex, filiation, language, family reconstruction, provenance, present
location, publication, text number, and the Sokoloff concordance number.

The complete recovered sample is held privately and is not in this repository.
Anyone wanting the withheld material should approach Dr Saar directly
(Utrecht University); she compiled it, and the full workbook is hers to share.

## Caveats

- Names from Syriac and Mandaic sources are transliterated into Hebrew script in the
  original; that convention is preserved here.
- `bowl_no_in_sokoloff` uses `0` for "not in Sokoloff", as the original did.
- These are the archive's own values, unverified against the editions they cite.
