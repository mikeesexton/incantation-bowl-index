# Schwab 1891 Dieulafoy/Susiana cohort

Primary-source review established three separately described incantation bowls from the French Dieulafoy mission excavations in Susiana. Schwab labels them N, O, and P; Montgomery's 1913 retrospective bibliography independently preserves the same three-letter sequence and collection attribution.
