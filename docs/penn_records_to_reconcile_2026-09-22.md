# Penn Museum records to reconcile

Prepared 22 September 2026 for follow-up with Katy Blanchard.

## Scope

Bowlam's forty main Montgomery/Penn number concordances are already checked:
each current Penn page displays the expected object number and PBS III text
number. The questions below are narrower catalogue-interpretation issues where
Penn staff may know the history behind a field or cross-reference.

The shortest useful version is **four question groups involving eight priority
records**. A lower-priority measurement question covers seven records, three of
which already appear in the priority list.

## Priority questions

### 1. B16007 and B16081: what does “Duplicate of” mean?

- [B16007](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/329808) —
  Montgomery/PBS III text 7. Its description says `Duplicate of16081`.
- [B16081](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/316265) — the
  apparent target of that reference.

The records describe different numbers of fragments and inscription lines, so
I have kept them as separate objects. Does “Duplicate of” mean the same physical
object, a duplicate or parallel inscription, an old catalogue cross-reference,
or something else? And is `16081` intended to mean object B16081?

### 2. B16086, B16062, and B6354: what is the scope of “Same as”?

- [B16086](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/161879) —
  Montgomery/PBS III text 32. Its description says `Same as B16062\B6354`.
- [B16062](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/287815) — says
  `Same as B16086`.
- [B6354](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/68943) — described
  as a sherd belonging to a larger fragment group that includes B16062.

Do these notes mean that the numbers refer to parts of one reconstructed bowl,
to catalogue aliases for one physical object, to texts judged identical or
parallel, or to another relationship? I have preserved the cross-references but
have not merged the records.

### 3. B2963: source of the “ca. 200 BCE” date

- [B2963](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/40422) —
  Montgomery/PBS III text 3. The online record gives `Date Made: ca. 200 BCE`.

This is much earlier than the late-antique dating normally associated with the
Nippur incantation-bowl group. Is the date intentional, and is there a catalogue
source or curatorial note behind it? I am not asking Penn to choose a chronology;
I would simply like to cite the source or history of the database value correctly.

### 4. B9010 and B9008: meaning of “Hebrew Language”

- [B9010](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/271621) —
  Montgomery/PBS III text 9. Penn gives `Inscription Language: Hebrew Language`;
  the edition-based description is Jewish Babylonian Aramaic.
- [B9008](https://collections.penn.museum/collections/object/222626) —
  Montgomery/PBS III text 31. Penn gives `Inscription Language: Hebrew Language`;
  published descriptions identify the bowl as Syriac, while the Penn prose also
  uses the historical term `Proto-Manichean`.

Does “Hebrew Language” here refer literally to the language, to Hebrew script,
or to a broader legacy catalogue category? Knowing how Penn intends this field
would let Bowlam report the records without turning a terminology difference
into a false scholarly disagreement.

for seven bowls. Penn labels its value **Outside Diameter**, whereas Montgomery

## Suggested order for a short conversation

Start with the two relationship questions, because Penn's catalogue vocabulary
may resolve them immediately. Then ask about the B2963 date and the meaning of
“Hebrew Language.” Treat the measurements as optional if time permits.

This is not a request to review Bowlam's entire Penn dataset. It is a bounded
list of places where understanding Penn's own field history or terminology would
materially improve the index.
