01 / A world written in clay

Protection,
close to home.

Incantation bowls carried written spells intended to protect people and their households from harm. Their everyday material holds traces of deeply personal concerns.

Late antiquity · c. fifth–seventh centuries CE

Associated especially with Mesopotamia, in the region of present-day Iraq and neighboring Iran, the bowls belong to a world that changed with the rise of Islam.

Editorial draft: the date range, geographic summary, and wording about when the practice ended await source citations. Research is paused.
